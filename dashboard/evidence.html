The latest major does most of the work. Online improves it a little.

The important result is the gap between Online-only and in-person-based methods. The precise best blend is a smaller, less certain optimisation problem.

What survives the robustness checks?

Long-tail deck noise is not driving the result

When the actual top 20 decks are kept individually and the long tail is grouped into Other as a diagnostic, the ordering is unchanged: blend > latest major > Online only.

The exact curve is not settled

The in-sample best blend reaches 84.01%, but walk-forward tuning did not beat the existing production decay rule. Treat the formula parameters as exploratory.
